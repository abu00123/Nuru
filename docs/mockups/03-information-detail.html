Education Kirundo, Burundi

Primary Schools Reopened in Kirundo Province

✓ Verified Last checked: 15 March 2024

Information

As of early 2024, all 14 public primary schools in Kirundo Province have reopened. Returning families can enroll children by presenting a birth certificate or sworn statement at the local school office. The Ministry of Education has confirmed that no fees are required for primary enrollment. School supplies are available through UNICEF distribution points in Kirundo commune.
